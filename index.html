Company Background

Trusted Industrial Material Handling Partner in Rajasthan

Established in 2017 in Jaipur, Om Industrial Solution has emerged as a premier manufacturer, wholesaler, and trader of heavy-duty material handling machinery, warehousing equipment, hydraulic pumps, and industrial trolleys under our flagship brand Stock Easy.

Led by our founder and proprietor Mr. Jagdish Prashad Swami, our business operates with a customer-centric ethos focused on heavy-gauge fabrication, dependable hydraulic components, and rapid Pan-India logistics. We maintain our manufacturing and storage facilities at Ramchandrapura Industrial Area and a sales branch in Jaichandpura, Sanganer, Jaipur.

  • 1-Year Service Warranty: Every hydraulic pump, cylinder, and stacker mechanism is tested for load safety and backed by our in-house service team.
  • In-House Machine Repairing & Spares: We recondition hydraulic hand pallet trucks, repair pumps, and maintain dedicated stocks of PU/nylon rollers and release valves in Jaipur.
  • Custom Fabrication on Order: Custom fork lengths (such as 1800mm extra-long), paper reel cradles, drum attachments, and special trolley platform dimensions.
